Capture an invoice

Place the paper on a flat, well-lit surface and keep all four edges in the frame.

1. Phone browser photo

Open this page on the phone and take a photo. Full camera resolution, so this gives the best accuracy. No app needed.

2. Phone camera over Wi-Fi

Open DroidCam (port 4747) or IP Webcam (port 8080, press Start server) on the phone. Its screen shows the Wi-Fi IP. Enter it once; it is remembered. Phone and PC must be on the same network. If the DroidCam PC client is running, Connect uses its picture automatically (no "busy", no green lines).

    Hold the phone still. The app focuses and keeps the sharpest frame. Video is often lower resolution than a photo (for example 640×480), so let the invoice fill the picture.

    3. DroidCam PC client / webcam

    Best way to use DroidCam on Windows: open the DroidCam client on this PC, connect it to the phone (you see the video there), then press Connect. The client gets the normal picture and gives it to this app as the "DroidCam Video" camera. Any webcam works here too.
