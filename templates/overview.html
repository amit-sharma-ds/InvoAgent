How invoices flow through this app

  1. Capture: upload PDFs / photos / ZIPs, import a folder, or use the phone camera.
  2. Extract: number, dates, seller, buyer, GSTINs, line items, tax and totals are read automatically.
  3. Check: totals must add up, GSTINs must be valid, CGST must equal SGST, the same invoice must not come twice.
  4. Review: anything doubtful lands in the Review queue. Correct values, then approve or reject.
  5. Export: the Data sheet gives all data as Excel / CSV for accounting.
