Add invoices

Take a phone photo, paste a copied picture, or add PDFs, photos, a ZIP or a whole folder. A pasted picture is read straight away; one invoice shows its result right away, more are read in the background.

The invoices are kept in this company's folder on the Companies page. Left empty, each invoice goes to the known company whose name or GSTIN it shows.

Phone camera

Open this page on your phone and tap Take photo: the phone's camera opens in the browser, no app needed. Full camera resolution, so this gives the best accuracy.

    For the best results

    • Phone photo: paper flat, good light, no shadow, all four edges in the picture. Take several photos one after another; they are all listed before uploading.
    • One invoice per photo or PDF (a PDF may have several pages).
    • Pick the company first: the invoices go straight into its folder. Choosing a folder whose name is the company fills the box for you.
    • The same file is never read twice, and a second copy of a saved invoice is marked as a duplicate.
    • Thousands of files: use the Scanner folder or the folder import command (see README).
